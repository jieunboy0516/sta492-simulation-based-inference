Some students are wondering: with only 15 minutes, are you expected to present all five AI-generated research directions, in addition to the assigned papers?

No! You can focus on the two or three directions you find most interesting or promising. The goal is to explain the research problem, the key ideas from the assigned papers, and your critical evaluation of the directions you select.

Briefly acknowledge the other directions, but you don't need to discuss all five in detail. Save the full evaluation for your written report.

As always, use your judgment about what is most important to communicate within the time available. That is part of the exercise! After all, most conference talks are only 10–15 minutes long.
